Privacy Policy

How we collect, use and protect the information you share with us.

Elite Visa Consultants respects the privacy of clients and applicants.

Information we may collect

  • Name & contact details
  • Passport & travel information
  • Employment & educational information
  • Financial & family information
  • Visa history
  • Documents provided by the client

We use information only for legitimate business purposes connected with the requested service and applicable legal obligations.

Document security

Clients should provide sensitive documents only through the secure channels designated by Elite Visa Consultants. We do not encourage clients to send unnecessary personal or financial information through public social-media comments.

Third-party services

Where an application requires submission through an embassy, consulate, government portal, visa application centre, university or other third party, relevant information may need to be provided to that organization as part of the application process.

Important: Elite Visa Consultants does not make visa decisions. Decisions are made by the relevant government authority, embassy, consulate or immigration authority. We do not guarantee visa approval.
