Germany

Germany Study Visa Assistance

Everything Indian students need to study in Germany: visa steps, documents, costs, universities and a free downloadable guide.

At a glance

Study in Germany: Key Facts

Germany offers low-cost or tuition-free education at public universities in most states. Studying there needs a national (Type D) student visa, which converts into a residence permit after arrival.

Visa type

National visa (Type D) for study purposes, converted to a residence permit in Germany.

Visa fee

€75 (paid in INR at the appointment), plus the VFS Global service fee.

Processing time

Plan for up to 12 weeks, especially in peak season (May–August).

Work while studying

Up to 140 full days or 280 half days per year, plus student assistant jobs at the university.

After graduation

Up to 18 months' residence permit to look for a job matching your degree.

Proof of funds

Blocked account with €11,904 for one year (€992 per month), a scholarship, or a formal sponsor declaration.

Choose your route

Which Study Visa Fits You?

Student Visa (Bachelor's / Master's)

For students with an admission letter from a German university. APS certificate is mandatory for Indian students.

Preparatory Course (Studienkolleg)

A 1-year course for 12th-pass students whose Indian school certificate does not give direct entry to a German Bachelor's. Ends with the Feststellungsprüfung. Usually requires German B1 or higher.

Student Applicant Visa

Up to 9 months in Germany to apply, take entrance tests or wait for admission. Converts to a student permit once admitted.

Language Course Visa

Intensive German course (at least 18 hours/week), usually up to 1 year. Work is generally not allowed. Courses under 90 days use a Schengen visa.

Do I need a Studienkolleg? Indian 12th certificates alone generally do not give direct Bachelor's admission. Direct entry is usually possible only with, for example, one or two years of a related Bachelor's in India or a qualifying JEE Advanced result — it depends on the subject and university, so check the course requirements first.

How it works

Buy cover valid from your arrival date until you enrol.

After the pre-check, attend with originals; biometrics taken and €75 fee paid.

08

Collect Visa

Check the visa sticker, then book flights and accommodation.

09

Arrive in Germany

Anmeldung, enrolment, bank account and residence permit.

Documentation

Student Visa Document Checklist

Bring originals and two sets of copies on A4 paper, in checklist order. Names and dates must match your passport exactly; documents not in English or German need a certified translation.

  • Valid passport (issued within 10 years, valid 3+ months after departure, 2 blank pages) and data-page copies
  • 2 recent biometric photos (35 x 45 mm, light background)
  • Completed online application and signed declarations
  • University admission letter
  • APS certificate
  • Blocked account confirmation / scholarship letter / Verpflichtungserklärung
  • Health insurance certificate
  • 10th and 12th mark sheets and certificates
  • Bachelor's certificate and semester-wise transcripts (Master's applicants)
  • Language certificate (IELTS/TOEFL or German, as the course requires)
  • CV (signed and dated) and motivation letter
  • Proof of semester contribution payment (if already paid)
  • Proof of accommodation in Germany (if available)

Germany does not accept apostilles on Indian public documents. For civil-status documents, the mission may run its own verification, which can take extra time. If spellings differ between documents and passport, get an affidavit or "one-and-the-same" certificate.

Key requirements

APS Certificate & Blocked Account

APS certificate

Verifies that Indian academic documents are genuine and is required for university applications and the student visa for almost all Indian students. Fee ₹18,000; about 3–4 weeks normally, up to 3 months in peak season. Semester-wise mark sheets are needed — a consolidated mark sheet alone is not accepted. Always read the latest notices on aps-india.de.

Blocked account (Sperrkonto)

A special German account where you deposit the full amount before the visa, then withdraw a fixed amount monthly after arrival. Open it online with a provider such as Expatrio or Fintiba, transfer via your bank (keep the receipt), download the confirmation and add it to your visa file. A scholarship letter or sponsor obligation letter can also be accepted as proof of funds.

Common mistakes to avoid

Applying for APS too late, which delays both university and visa applications • a blocked account below the current requirement • unexplained gaps in education or work history • booking flights or paying rent deposits before the visa is approved.

The visa interview

Expect simple questions: why Germany and this course, how it connects to your past studies, how you're financing your stay and your plans afterwards. Keep answers honest and consistent with your motivation letter and CV.

After you arrive

Register your address (Anmeldung) within 14 days, activate your blocked account and open a German bank account, switch to statutory health insurance before enrolment, and book your residence permit appointment at the Ausländerbehörde before your visa expires.

Good to know

Germany Study Visa FAQ

Is a German study visa guaranteed if I have an admission letter?

No. The admission letter is one required document. The visa decision is made by the German mission, based on your full application including funds, insurance and APS.

Do I need German to study in Germany?

Not for English-taught programmes, where an IELTS/TOEFL score is typically needed. German-taught programmes usually require TestDaF, DSH or Goethe C1; Studienkolleg usually needs about B1.

Can I study a Bachelor's after 12th directly?

Usually not directly — most Indian 12th students need a Studienkolleg first. Rules vary by subject and university, so check the programme's admission requirements.

Is the guide's information final?

It was checked in October 2026. Fees, blocked-account amounts and rules are usually updated each January, so always confirm on the official websites before applying.
