Plan ahead

Visa Document Checklist

Every visa application is different. Your checklist is based on visa category, destination, nationality, residence, employment status, financial circumstances, travel purpose, family circumstances and previous travel/visa history.

Identity

Passport, photographs, identification documents.

Financial

Bank statements, income evidence, tax documents, sponsorship evidence.

Employment

Employment contract, employer letter, salary documents, leave approval.

Business

Business registration, tax documents, company bank statements, invitation documents.

Family

Marriage certificate, birth certificate, relationship evidence.

Travel

Itinerary, accommodation, insurance, invitation letter where applicable.

Additional

Translations, apostille/authentication, previous visa or refusal documents.

More than a form

Document Verification

A visa application is more than an application form. Our review focuses on the overall consistency of the application — your documents should tell a clear, consistent and truthful story.

  • Passport & personal details
  • Employment & salary documents
  • Bank statements & tax documents
  • Business documents
  • Educational documents
  • Accommodation & travel plans
  • Invitation & sponsorship documents
  • Marriage & birth certificates
  • Travel insurance
  • Previous visa history & refusal documents
  • Translations & supporting statements
Already refused?

Visa Refusal Review

A refusal does not automatically mean every future application will be refused. The first step is understanding the refusal.

  • Refusal reason
  • Financial evidence
  • Purpose of travel
  • Supporting documentation
  • Employment / business evidence
  • Travel history
  • Inconsistencies & missing documents
Important: We cannot guarantee that an appeal or new application will be successful. Our role is to help identify documentary and procedural issues and prepare the application based on the available evidence.

Frequently Asked Questions

Can you guarantee my visa?

No. The final decision is made by the relevant embassy, consulate or immigration authority.

Do I need to use a visa consultant?

Not necessarily. Applicants can generally apply directly where the relevant authority permits. Our service is for people who want professional assistance with documentation and application preparation.

Can you get an earlier appointment?

We cannot guarantee appointment availability or priority treatment. Appointments are controlled by the relevant official system or visa application centre.

Can you guarantee visa approval if I pay more?

No. We do not sell guaranteed approvals.

Do you provide fake documents?

Absolutely not. We only work with genuine documents and truthful applicant information.

Can you help after a refusal?

Yes. We can review the refusal and identify areas that may require attention for an appeal or future application.

Do government fees come with your service fee?

Not unless specifically stated in your quotation. Government, embassy, consular, visa-centre, translation, insurance and other third-party costs may be separate.

How long does a visa take?

Processing times vary by country, visa category, season, application volume and individual circumstances. Always check the current official source.

Can you prepare applications for children?

Yes. We can provide a separate document checklist for minor applicants.

Can I apply without going through your company?

Yes. Our service is optional professional assistance. Applicants remain responsible for their application.

Important: Elite Visa Consultants does not make visa decisions. Decisions are made by the relevant government authority, embassy, consulate or immigration authority. We do not guarantee visa approval.
